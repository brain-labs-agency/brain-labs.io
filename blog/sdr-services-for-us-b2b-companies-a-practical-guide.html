01What are SDR services?

SDR services are outbound prospecting done for you by an outside team. An SDR, or sales development representative, focuses on finding potential buyers, moving them through the sales pipeline, and qualifying them before passing them to the people who close deals. As TechTarget explains in its definition of a sales development representative, an SDR prospects and qualifies leads, then hands them over to closers.

That handoff is the point. Closers can spend their time on the leads most likely to convert instead of working every name on a list. A key SDR task is also filtering out contacts with no buying interest, so the calendar fills with conversations that have a real chance to move forward.

Outsourced SDR services move this day-to-day prospecting work to an outside team. You keep the final sales conversation, while the provider handles the research, outreach, replies, and scheduling that come before it.

For a founder or sales leader, the real question is not whether this work needs to get done. It is whether you want to build, train, and manage a team to do it yourself.

02How does outsourced outbound work step by step?

BrainLabs runs the full outbound process, so you do not have to assemble the pieces yourself. The work covers these steps:

  • Market and ideal customer mapping: the team defines who your buyers are before any message goes out.
  • Domain and inbox setup: domains and mailboxes are configured and warmed up. This takes about three weeks and protects deliverability before email campaigns start.
  • Multichannel outreach: LinkedIn, email, and calls to verified direct numbers.
  • Reply handling and meeting booking: replies are read and sorted, and qualified meetings are scheduled.

AI agents support the work. Scout looks for companies with buying signals, Lens researches each account, Quill drafts emails, Relay sends them at a safe pace, Echo reads and sorts replies, and Atlas prepares a summary of each booked meeting.

A dedicated Agent Base manager, a real person, reads and approves every email before it is sent. Nothing goes out on autopilot.

06What should you ask before you start?

Before you agree to any outsourced outbound program, ask specific questions that show how the work will be done. A good provider should answer them clearly.

  • How do you choose the channel for each buyer: phone, LinkedIn, or email?
  • Who reviews and approves each email before it is sent?
  • What will I see in the dashboard, and in real time or only in reports?
  • What does the setup period include, and what happens during those three weeks?
  • How are replies handled, and who decides which conversations go to a person?
  • What reports will I receive, and how often?
  • How is the engagement structured, and how much notice is needed to change it?

Pay attention to the answers. Specific, step-by-step answers suggest a team that understands the work. Vague promises about volume or timing are a warning sign, and BrainLabs does not guarantee meetings, sales, or revenue.

08Frequently asked questions

Is everything done by AI?

No. AI agents research accounts, draft emails, and sort replies, but an Agent Base manager reads and approves every email before it is sent. Replies that need a person are handled by a person.

What can I see in the dashboard?

You can see each lead found, each email sent, each reply, and each meeting booked, at the moment it happens. You do not have to wait for the weekly report to check progress. The dashboard is for viewing the work, not for editing or sending emails.

How long before I see results?

Expect about three weeks of setup and warm-up before email campaigns start, which protects deliverability. After that, qualified conversations often begin during the first month of active prospecting. BrainLabs does not guarantee a specific number of meetings or a fixed timeline.
