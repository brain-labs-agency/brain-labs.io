What is SDR as a Service, and when does it make sense?

By the BL Agency team6 min read
In this articleThe short definitionWhat it includesVersus hiring an SDRSigns you are ready

Most B2B companies grow on referrals until the referrals slow down. At that point the usual answer is to hire a sales development rep. SDR as a Service is the other option: you hand the whole outbound function to a team that already runs it.

The short definition

SDR as a Service means an outside team builds and runs your outbound for you. Instead of hiring, training and managing your own SDRs, you get the research, the outreach, the reply handling and the booked meetings as a service. Your own team only shows up for the sales calls.

What it usually includes

  • Market mapping and a clear ideal customer profile
  • Domains and mailboxes set up and warmed before anything is sent
  • Outreach by email, LinkedIn and phone, with messaging written for each channel
  • Reply handling, qualification and follow up
  • Meetings booked on your calendar and regular reporting
The goal is not more activity. It is more qualified conversations.

Versus hiring an SDR

Hiring in house gives you a person who learns your product deeply, but it is slow and risky if the outbound system has never been tested. A service starts from a working system and lets you see what outbound produces for your offer before you build a team around it.

Signs you are ready

  1. You sell a B2B offer worth $2K or more per month or project.
  2. You know who your best clients are.
  3. Referrals alone no longer fill the pipeline.
  4. You want to close deals, not manage an outbound team.
