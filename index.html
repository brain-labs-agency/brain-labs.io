Questions people ask first

Before you decide.

Is it all AI?

No. The agents do the research, the drafting and the sorting. A dedicated Agent Base manager reads and approves every email before it is sent, and handles the replies that need a person.

What can I see on the dashboard?

Every lead the agents find, every email sent, every reply and every meeting booked, as it happens. You don't have to wait for a weekly report.

How long before we see results?

We start with about three weeks of setup and email warm up to protect deliverability. Campaigns go live after that, and qualified conversations usually start in the first month of active outreach. Calling can start before email does.

What makes BL different from other outbound agencies?

Most agencies buy a list and run the same sequence on everyone. We qualify reachability first and research each account, so the channel and the message fit the buyer. And you can watch the work live.

Do you work with companies outside the US?

Yes. Our team has run outbound for buyers in North America, Europe and Latin America.

Can I hire a rep instead?

Yes. We also place bilingual sales professionals from Latin America who work US business hours. Tell us the role and we come back with three profiles in 72 hours.
