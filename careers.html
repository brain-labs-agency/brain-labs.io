Who we are

Not a recruiting agency.

BL Agency runs outbound for B2B companies. We build the systems, write the sequences and make the calls. Placing salespeople came out of that work: companies kept asking us who they should hire.

The person interviewing you has done the job you are applying for. You are evaluated on what you can do, and if you are not ready yet, we tell you what would make you ready.

What we look for

Four things, and one of them isn't your CV.

  • Conversational EnglishAble to hold a call when someone pushes back.
  • Real sales experienceOutbound, SDR work, appointment setting or B2B selling, with your own numbers.
  • US business hoursMost roles run on Eastern Time.
  • A reliable setupStable internet and a quiet place to take calls.

How it works

Apply once. We come to you when there's a fit.

  1. Apply
