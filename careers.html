Who we are

Not a recruiting agency.

BL Agency runs outbound for B2B companies. We build the systems, write the sequences, make the calls. Placing salespeople came out of that work — companies kept asking us who they should hire.

That means the person interviewing you has done the job you're applying for. You'll be evaluated on what you can actually do, not on how your CV is formatted — and if you're not ready yet, we'll tell you what would make you ready instead of leaving you guessing.

What we look for

Four things, and one of them isn't your CV.

The interview is twenty minutes with a working account executive. Part of it is in English, part of it is a live roleplay. You'll know how it went before it ends.

  • Conversational English. Not "advanced" on a CV — able to hold a call when someone pushes back.
  • Real sales experience. Outbound, SDR work, appointment setting or B2B selling, with your own numbers attached.
  • Availability during US business hours. Most roles run on Eastern Time.
  • A reliable setup. Stable internet and a quiet place to take calls.
