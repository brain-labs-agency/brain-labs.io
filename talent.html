Questions

What US companies ask before hiring nearshore.

Contractor or employee?

Whichever fits your company. Most engagements run as contractors invoicing through us, so you get one monthly invoice and no payroll overhead. If you would rather hire directly, we structure it that way. We agree the model before you interview anyone.

How good is their English, really?

Every candidate is interviewed in English by our sales team and put through a live cold call roleplay before they enter our pool. You also interview them yourself before deciding.

What if the rep doesn't work out?

We replace them at no additional cost within the first 30 days. After that the engagement runs month to month with 30 days notice on either side.

Can we hire them directly later?

Yes. There is a conversion fee, agreed in the contract, that turns the placement into a direct hire.

How is this different from an offshore staffing firm?

We only place sales roles, and the screening is done by people who build revenue systems for a living.
