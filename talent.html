Our approach

A recruiter can't tell you whether someone can sell.

Most staffing firms match keywords. Someone reads a CV, sees "SDR" and "outbound," and forwards it. Nobody on their side has ever built a pipeline, handled an objection, or owned a number — so nobody on their side can tell the difference between a candidate who sounds good and one who sells.

BL Agency is a sales and consulting firm. We build and operate outbound systems for B2B companies, and we've done it for more than a hundred of them. Every candidate we present is assessed against the standard we hold our own operators to.

What you get back is a short list — not thirty CVs — with a clear note on where each person is strong and where they aren't. And when we don't have the right fit for a role, we'd rather tell you than send someone to fill the slot.

FAQ

What US companies ask before hiring nearshore.

How do we engage them — contractor or employee?

Whichever fits your company. Most engagements run as contractors invoicing through us, so you get a single monthly invoice and no payroll or employer-of-record overhead. If you'd rather bring someone on directly, we structure it that way instead. We agree the model before you interview anyone.

How good is their English, really?

Every candidate is interviewed in English by our sales team and put through a live cold-call roleplay before they enter our pool. If they can't hold a conversation under pressure with a prospect pushing back, they don't get sent to you. You'll also interview them yourself before deciding.

What if the rep doesn't work out?

We replace them at no additional cost within the first 30 days. After that, the engagement runs month to month with 30 days' notice on either side.

Can we hire them directly later?

Yes — we'll talk about it. There's a conversion fee, agreed in the contract, that converts the placement into a direct hire. What we ask is that it goes through us rather than around us.

Do they work our hours or theirs?

Yours. Latin America overlaps the US business day across every time zone, so we scope the role to the hours you need covered and staff it accordingly — not the other way around.

How is this different from an offshore staffing firm?

Two things: we only place sales roles, and the screening is done by people who build revenue systems for a living. A generalist staffing firm can place a developer, a designer and an SDR from the same process. We can't — and that's the point.
